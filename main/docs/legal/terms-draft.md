# Kody Terms — short draft

**Unpublished draft, 18 October 2026. This is proposed text for review, not an agreement currently accepted by users.**

Kody helps you practise programming through games, lessons and coding challenges.

- **Your account:** Use an email you control, choose an appropriate username and keep your password private. Your username identifies you publicly. Personal profile names are optional. Do not impersonate someone or share an account to bypass limits.
- **Play fairly:** Do not attack the service, upload harmful files, harass others, cheat reward systems or try to access another person's private information. Practice previews do not earn saved progress; the server checks eligible completions.
- **Creating content:** Upload only material you own or have permission to use. Give required attribution. You keep ownership; the proposed permission is for Kody to store, display and deliver your submitted material as needed to provide the learning service. Publication and role applications require review.
- **Moderation:** Staff may reject, withdraw or restrict content and accounts under the platform's rules. If you think a decision was mistaken, contact kody.support@gmail.com. An appeal does not automatically reverse a decision.
- **Progress and availability:** Learning activities are practice, not a promise of qualifications or employment. Features and availability may change. Purchases, payouts and external execution are unavailable while their live setup is deferred; the wallet is not a bank account.
- **Leaving:** Use account settings to request deletion. Necessary financial, security and shared-content records can require separate handling; the Privacy notice explains the limits.
- **Questions:** Contact kody.support@gmail.com. Changes to published terms will need a dated version and a clear notice.

Before publication, identify the legal operator and postal address, approve eligibility/age and geographic scope, and review creator permissions and consumer rights. No governing law, minimum age, liability waiver or consent history has been invented. Keep these editorial notes out of the eventual published agreement.
