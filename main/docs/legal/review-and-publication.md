# Simple legal drafts: review and publication

The owner requested short, plain Terms and Privacy on 2026-10-18. The drafts are original Kody text; they are intentionally not HTTP routes or registration checkboxes. Keeping them simple does not supply the operator's missing legal identity. Registration acceptance is **not implemented or claimed**.

Publication requires the actual operator and postal contact, approved age/geographic eligibility, retention schedule and enabled processing details. Recommended initial audience is an adult Philippines pilot, but this has not been made an age gate or a claim about current users. Do not delete unverified accounts, release usernames or expire creator credentials to invent a retention policy; changed-email Unverified accounts can have substantial retained history.

After review, publish immutable dated versions, link them beside an unchecked required Terms acknowledgment, validate the server-owned version and acceptance in the registration transaction, retain minimal acceptance evidence and decide how existing users see material changes. Privacy information must remain a notice; do not conflate it with optional marketing consent. A draft cannot be used as accepted legal text.
